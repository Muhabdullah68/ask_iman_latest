# Privacy Policy for Ask Iman

**Effective Date: June 05, 2026**

Ask Iman ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Ask Iman mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information in our App. Our primary goal is to provide Islamic lifestyle tools while respecting your privacy.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

Ask Iman is designed to function with minimal data collection. We do not require you to create an account or provide personal identification to use the core features of the App.

### Types of Data Handled

#### Location Data
To provide accurate prayer times (Salat) and Qibla direction, the App requires access to your device's location. 
* **How we use it:** Location data is processed locally on your device to calculate timings based on your coordinates.
* **Storage:** We do not store your movement history or transmit your precise location to our servers.
* **Control:** You can enable or disable location permissions at any time through your device settings. If disabled, you may need to enter your city manually to get prayer times.

#### Device Information
We may collect non-identifiable technical information such as device model and operating system version to help us optimize the App's performance and fix bugs.

## 2. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services which may collect information used to identify you:
* **Google Play Services:** Used for app functionality and updates.
* **Firebase Analytics:** Used to understand how the app is used (e.g., which features are most popular) in an anonymous and aggregated format.

## 3. Data Security

We value your trust in providing us your information. We use industry-standard security measures to protect the limited data handled by the App. However, please remember that no method of transmission over the internet or electronic storage is 100% secure.

## 4. Children's Privacy

Our App does not collect any personal information from children. It is safe for use by individuals of all ages as a tool for religious practice.

## 5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. You are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. Changes are effective immediately after they are posted on this page.
